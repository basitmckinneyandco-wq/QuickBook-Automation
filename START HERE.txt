===============================================================
  QuickBooks Automation Dashboard  -  first-time setup
===============================================================

You do this ONCE. After that it is one double-click, every day.


---------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1   Install Python
---------------------------------------------------------------

Click the Start button, type   powershell   and open it.
Copy this line in, press Enter, and wait for it to finish:

    winget install Python.Python.3.13

Then CLOSE PowerShell and open it again. (It has to notice the
new Python.)


---------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2   Install the one thing it needs
---------------------------------------------------------------

In PowerShell, copy in these two lines, one at a time.
Change the folder path if you unzipped somewhere else:

    cd "C:\QuickBooks Automation"

    pip install -r requirements.txt

You do NOT need to download anything from Intuit. QuickBooks
already installs the part that lets this app talk to it.


---------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2b  Put the Google key file in this folder
---------------------------------------------------------------

The dashboard reads and writes four columns in the Google Sheet -
QB Customer/Job, QB Premium, QB Commission, QB Fees - so that
everyone working the sheet can see what has already been done.

To do that it needs a key file called:

    service-account.json

It is NOT in this zip, on purpose: it is a password, and passwords
do not travel in a file everybody has a copy of. Ask whoever sent
you this folder for it, and put it here, next to Start Dashboard.cmd.

Without it the dashboard still runs and still creates things in
QuickBooks - only the four status columns stop working, in both
directions.


---------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3   Put an icon on your Desktop
---------------------------------------------------------------

In this folder, double-click:

    Create Desktop Shortcut.cmd

An icon called "QuickBooks Dashboard" appears on your Desktop.
That is what you use from now on.


---------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4   Start it
---------------------------------------------------------------

1.  Open your company in QuickBooks Desktop first.

2.  Double-click the "QuickBooks Dashboard" icon.
    A black window opens and your browser opens the dashboard.

    LEAVE THE BLACK WINDOW OPEN. That window IS the app.
    Closing it stops everything.

3.  Watch QuickBooks. A blue box appears asking for permission.
    Choose:

        "Yes, whenever my QuickBooks company file is open"

    then click Continue.

    IMPORTANT: if you close that box instead of answering it,
    QuickBooks remembers it as a REFUSAL and nothing will work
    afterwards. To undo that: in QuickBooks go to
    Edit > Preferences > Integrated Applications >
    Company Preferences, select "QB Automation Dashboard",
    click Remove, then start the dashboard again.


---------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5   Point it at your company
---------------------------------------------------------------

In the dashboard, click  Status  on the left.

Under "Company file", pick your company from the list - it will
say "open in QuickBooks" next to it - and press
"Switch to this company".

If your company is not in the list, press Rescan.


---------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 6   Check before you send anything
---------------------------------------------------------------

Still on the Status page, press "Run check".

This looks inside YOUR company file for every account, template
and item the invoices need, and tells you which are missing.
A company that has not been set up yet will fail most of them.

Do not send invoices until this is all green.


===============================================================
  EVERY DAY AFTER THAT
===============================================================

1.  Open your company in QuickBooks.
2.  Double-click the "QuickBooks Dashboard" icon on your Desktop.

That is all.


===============================================================
  TWO THINGS THAT CAUSE MOST PROBLEMS
===============================================================

CLOSE THE CUSTOMER CENTRE in QuickBooks before you send.
That window holds the customer list open, and writing to it
underneath can crash QuickBooks in the middle of a batch.

NEVER USE "RUN AS ADMINISTRATOR".
Open PowerShell normally - click Start, type powershell, click it.
If you run it as administrator but QuickBooks normally, Windows
keeps them apart and the app cannot see QuickBooks at all. It
then says "QuickBooks does not have a company file open" even
though your company is clearly on screen.

DO NOT CLICK INSIDE THE BLACK WINDOW.
Windows freezes it when you click in it. If the dashboard seems
stuck, click that window and press the Esc key.


===============================================================
  IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
===============================================================

Two pages answer nearly everything:

  Status   - can I send? What is connected, what is missing.
  History  - what happened when I did? Every action, and the
             exact error, which you can copy and send on.

More detail is in SETUP.md in this folder.
